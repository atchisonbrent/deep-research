# Product landscape

## Choose this mode when

The goal is durable understanding of a product category: technologies in play, major option families, the price/performance frontier, reliability and owner experience, repairability, ecosystem lock-in, premium alternatives, and where the category is heading. For "what exact product should I buy now," load `product-buying-research`; for a choice among named options against requirements, use `comparative-analysis`. The two may be stacked when both layers matter.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. manufacturer specifications and documentation, by model and revision;
2. independent lab measurements with method (teardowns, instrumented tests, long-term reliability data);
3. aggregated owner and repair data with sampling visible;
4. expert reviewers who disclose test method and units;
5. retailer listings, for price-frontier snapshots at a stated date;
6. marketing and sponsored reviews, as claims.

## Decomposition pattern

Map the category into segments and option families. For each, record the technology basis, what differentiates families, typical price band at cutoff, known failure modes, and lock-in. Separate durable facts (technology, architecture, repairability) from perishable ones (price, availability, current model year) and label the latter for refresh.

## Mode gates

- Segments are defined by use or technology, not by brand marketing tiers.
- Reliability claims cite data with sample and period, or are marked anecdotal.
- Price-frontier statements carry date and region.

## Output sections

- Verdict
- Category map and segments
- Decision criteria that matter
- Option families and tradeoffs
- Value frontier and premium cases
- Reliability, repairability, and lock-in
- Category direction
- Purchase-time facts to refresh

## Completion criteria

Each segment and family is evidenced; durable and perishable claims are distinguished; the refresh list names what must be rechecked at purchase time.

## Pitfalls

- Ranking products when the task was to map the category.
- Letting current-year pricing masquerade as durable value.
- Treating a reviewer's favourite as the frontier.
