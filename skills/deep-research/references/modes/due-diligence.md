# Due diligence

## Choose this mode when

The research serves a specific decision with a materiality threshold—an investment, acquisition, partnership, vendor selection, or grant—and the deliverable is verified facts, unresolved representations, red flags, and go/no-go conditions. Use `company-research` for a general analysis without a transaction.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. primary legal, financial, and technical records: filings, contracts, audits, code, security assessments, ownership registries;
2. counterparty and customer evidence with identity confirmed;
3. litigation, regulatory, and enforcement records;
4. management representations, recorded as representations pending verification;
5. independent reporting;
6. reference calls and reputation, as attributed and interested.

## Decomposition pattern

Define the decision, the materiality threshold, and the red-flag list before retrieval. For each representation that matters: what is claimed, what record would verify it, whether that record was obtained, and what was found. Map dependencies and concentration (customers, suppliers, key people, platforms, licences). Build downside cases with the evidence that would trigger each.

## Mode gates

- Every material representation is verified, unresolved, or contradicted—never silently accepted.
- Absence of public evidence is not evidence of absence of risk; state what could not be checked.
- Go/no-go conditions are testable statements, not sentiments.

## Output sections

- Verdict and go/no-go conditions
- Decision, threshold, and red-flag list
- Verified facts
- Unresolved representations
- Red flags
- Dependency and concentration map
- Downside cases
- Evidence requests
- Gaps

## Completion criteria

Each material representation has a verification status; red flags cite records; downside cases name triggers; the evidence-request list covers every unresolved item.

## Pitfalls

- Letting management's data room define the question.
- Treating a clean public record as a clean record.
- Presenting a recommendation without its conditions.
